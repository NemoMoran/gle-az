# Reference review

Reviewed in a browser on 25 October 2026:
- https://gle.az/
- https://gle.az/services.html

The text-fetch tool returned HTTP 403, but the pages loaded in the browser. Observations below come from the rendered desktop pages. This was a visual/content review, not a full functional or mobile audit.

## What the reference contains

Green Life Engineering MMC is presented as a renewable energy and building-services business. The navigation includes home, about, services, training, contact and an external solar calculator. Azerbaijani and English language links are visible.

The homepage uses solar-panel and wind-energy photography, a large white logo area, a bright green navigation strip and a rotating hero with large centered headings. Several slides contain long explanatory paragraphs. Further down are photovoltaic content, services and a substantial footer with contact details, service links and a gallery.

Homepage topics include wind, hydroelectricity, geothermal energy, biomass, photovoltaic training, wind training and efficient pumps. These educational topics should not automatically be treated as confirmed installation services.

The services page explicitly shows photovoltaic installation, monitoring and panel cleaning, plus links for heating, cooling and ventilation. The footer also mentions after-sales support and warranty-related claims. Do not transfer those promises to a new German business without confirmation.

## Keep

- A clear renewable energy identity through real equipment and landscape photography.
- Photovoltaics as the central topic, supported by building efficiency services.
- Straightforward company, services, training and contact navigation.
- Easy access to a consultation request.

## Improve

- Replace the tall logo block and heavy navigation with one compact header.
- Replace the rotating, text-heavy hero with one deliberate composition and concise copy.
- Use a more restrained forest-green palette, generous spacing and stronger typographic hierarchy.
- Give every service a clear title and short practical explanation.
- Use meaningful links rather than the reference's numerous visible # destinations.
- Keep training discoverable without letting course descriptions overwhelm the homepage.
- Avoid decorative social links, a large image gallery and search unless they serve real content.

## German adaptation

Write original German copy with formal “Sie”. Use German navigation and field labels. Do not reuse the Azerbaijan address, phone number, company suffix, partner claims or contact email. Do not assume German registration, certifications, subsidies, nationwide coverage or savings guarantees. Include editable draft legal pages without claiming they are legally complete.

This is a design and content reference, not permission to reuse the original site's logo, photos or text. Use original or properly licensed assets.
